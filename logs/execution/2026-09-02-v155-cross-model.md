## Decision

The Qwen default-panel gain (`+0.000116536`, 4/0/164) is a Qwen-local low-recall coordinate signal,
not a robust architecture-independent improvement. The later official `16581 / 208.5s` result
confirmed rejection below v86; v155 remains a diagnostic control only. The
next Linear mechanism branches from the pre-A3 parent and must be a new single-pass deployment-
metric method (Weight-decoupled stored scale or block-Schur HiF4-GPTQ); no further permutation
threshold/layer-list sweep is justified.
